# BusinessCurrent post-launch roadmap

This backlog deliberately excludes the sellable V1 release. Items move into delivery only after customer validation, source review, and an explicit implementation plan.

- Websites
- Descriptions
- Founding years
- Ownership
- Parents and subsidiaries
- Executives

## Phase 3 — Capital intelligence

- Funding rounds
- Investors
- Acquisitions
- Valuations
- Debt
- Grants

## Phase 4 — Operational intelligence

- Hiring
- Property
- Leases
- Additional permits
- Additional licenses
- Additional locations

## Phase 5 — Intelligence platform

- Lists
- Comparisons
- Additional exports
- Public API
- CRM integrations
- Team workspaces
- Validated predictions

These capabilities are not part of the sellable V1 and must not be advertised as available until they are implemented and verified.
